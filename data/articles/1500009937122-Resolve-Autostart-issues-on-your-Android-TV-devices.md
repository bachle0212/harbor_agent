# Resolve Autostart issues on your Android TV devices

Article URL: https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500009937122-Resolve-Autostart-issues-on-your-Android-TV-devices
Article ID: 1500009937122
Updated: 2026-09-10T09:46:08Z

Auto Start feature is not working on your devices. The devices give example: Chromecast with Android TV, Mi Stick, HiSense, Sony, and other TV with Android TV

This article guides you on how to resolve the Auto Start feature on your Android device.

### **Let's jump in and get started:**

Go to Settings > Apps > Special app access > Display over other apps, make sure the OptiSigns app is allowed.

1. ) Go to **Setting**

![image](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/1500015378922)

2. Select **Apps**

![image](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/1500015697201)

3. Go to **Special app access**

![image](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/1500015697221)

4) Select **Display over other apps**

![image](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/1500015697241)

5. Make sure the OptiSigns app is **ON**

![image](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/1500015697261)

### **That's it.**

Your Auto Start feature will be worked.
