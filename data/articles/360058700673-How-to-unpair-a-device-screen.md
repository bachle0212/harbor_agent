# How to unpair a device, screen?

Article URL: https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360058700673-How-to-unpair-a-device-screen
Article ID: 360058700673
Updated: 2026-09-10T09:44:43Z

Sometimes you need to unpair a screens when you not using it anymore or to move to different account.

You can do so from the Portal or from the device.

**From Portal:**

You can simply remove the screen:

![image](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/360098754073)

**From device:**

you can open side menu (press the 3 bars button on FireStick Remote or press Enter on keyboard for Windows, Linux, Mac, etc.)

Then select Unpair

![image](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/360096443094)
